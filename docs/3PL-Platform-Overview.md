## The Modern Operating System for 3PL Warehouses

**Manage your warehouses. Delight your clients. Ship faster.**

---

## The Problem

Many 3PL teams today rely on multiple disconnected systems and manual workflows:

- Logging into different client storefronts (Shopify, Amazon, etc.) just to pull orders — handling client credentials, creating security liability
- Tracking inventory on spreadsheets that are outdated the moment they're saved
- Taking orders over email and phone — then manually entering them into separate systems
- Shipping across disconnected tools — one site for labels, another for tracking, another for rates
- Invoicing manually — spending hours at the end of every month calculating what to charge each client

This doesn't just slow things down — it creates errors, security risks, and limits how fast you can grow. Every new client adds more complexity instead of more profit.

---

## The Solution

A single, centralized platform with two dedicated interfaces:

- **Warehouse Dashboard** — Everything your team needs to receive inventory, manage stock, fulfill orders, and ship — across all your warehouse locations, all in one screen.
- **Client Portal** — A fully branded, self-service portal — with your logo and your identity — where your clients manage their own orders, inventory, and shipping. No more back-and-forth emails.

**Plus:** a built-in shipping engine — everything tools like Shippo and ShipStation offer (rate comparison, label generation, tracking) — but integrated directly into your warehouse workflow. No separate tools needed.

---

## How It Works

### Step 1 — Clients Send You Inventory

Your clients notify you when stock is on the way. Your team scans it in, verifies the count, and it's instantly live in the system — assigned to a bin, shelf, or zone at whichever warehouse location you choose.

*No more handwritten receiving logs. No more "did that shipment arrive?"*

### Step 2 — Orders Flow In Automatically

No need to log into your clients' Shopify, Amazon, or other marketplace accounts. Orders flow into the platform automatically through direct integrations — or clients submit them directly through their portal (one at a time or thousands via bulk upload).

Need custom details? Clients can attach personalization info to every order — engraving text, custom messages, color selections, artwork uploads, and more.

*No more accessing client accounts. No more credential sharing. No more manual data entry.*

### Step 3 — Your Team Fulfills

Your warehouse staff sees a clear pick queue on their screen or tablet. They go to the right bin, scan the right item, pack the box, and see any personalization details right on the packing screen. Step by step, error-free.

*No more guessing. No more mis-picks. No more missed custom requests.*

### Step 4 — Shipping Made Simple

Clients can upload their own shipping labels, or purchase labels directly through the platform. The system compares rates across major carriers to find the best price. Address validation catches bad addresses before they become failed deliveries.

*Upload a label or buy one. Either way — one click and it's done.*

### Step 5 — Everyone Stays Informed

The moment an order ships, your client gets tracking info automatically. They see every step — from "order received" to "delivered" — right in their portal. No phone calls, no emails.

*Your clients always know where their orders stand.*

### Step 6 — Billing Runs Itself

Every pick, every label, every pallet stored — tracked and ready to invoice. You set your own prices. Generate professional invoices with a single click. Set a balance threshold and the system collects payment automatically when it's reached.

*No more end-of-month billing nightmares. No more chasing payments.*

## Custom Pricing — You Set Your Own Rates

Every 3PL prices differently. This platform doesn't force a billing model — you define exactly what you charge and how much.

| What You Can Price | How You Set It |
|---|---|
| Price per order | Flat fee per order fulfilled — you set the amount |
| Pick fee | Charge per item picked, or per order — your choice |
| Packing fee | Charge for packing labor — flat rate or per item |
| Box / packaging costs | Set prices per box size (small, medium, large, custom) |
| Multi-item orders | Base fee + per-additional-item fee for orders with multiple products |
| Storage fees | Per pallet, per bin, per shelf — by day, week, or month |
| Receiving fees | Per unit received, per shipment, or per pallet |
| Shipping label markup | Add your margin on labels purchased through the platform |
| Returns processing | Per return handled |
| Special handling | Fragile, oversized, kitting, gift wrap — add any custom fee |
| Minimum order fees | Set minimum charges per client if needed |

**Per-client overrides** — give different clients different rates when needed (volume discounts, special agreements). The system tracks everything automatically, and invoices are generated with every line item broken down.

*Your pricing. Your rules. The system just tracks it and does the math.*
